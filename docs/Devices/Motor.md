# Motor

Motor extends from Device. It allows you to move something angularily. This can be used for wheels, arms, or more. It gives you the ability to adjust throttle, and access its angular position and velocity. 

Construction requires you to define its limits like max angular velocity and max angular acceleration. 

Either than limit construction you only have control over throttle from ```-1.0``` to ```1.0```. 1 being full forward and -1 being full backwards.
