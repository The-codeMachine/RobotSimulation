# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Robot Simulation a welcoming, respectful, and constructive experience for everyone.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, users, and community members to participate professionally and in good faith.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
* Discussing technical disagreements professionally.
* Focusing criticism on ideas, implementations, and decisions rather than individuals.
* Assuming good intentions while addressing disagreements.
* Respecting different levels of experience and backgrounds.
* Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
* Helping create an environment where people can ask questions without fear of ridicule.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
* Discrimination or derogatory comments directed at individuals or groups.
* Deliberate disruption of project discussions or development.
* Trolling or intentionally inflammatory behavior.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
* Threats of violence or other threatening behavior.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate or harmful in a professional open-source community.

## Technical Disagreement

Technical disagreement is a normal and valuable part of open-source development.

Contributors are encouraged to challenge designs, implementations, assumptions, and proposed changes. Strong disagreement about technical matters is acceptable when it remains focused on the work and is communicated respectfully.

A maintainer may reject a contribution because it does not fit the project's technical direction. Such a decision is not a personal judgment of the contributor.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

* Asking someone to modify their behavior.
* Removing comments or other contributions.
* Closing issues or pull requests.
* Temporarily restricting participation.
* Permanently banning participation in project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will be made based on the circumstances of each situation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the project maintainer.

Do not use public issues or pull requests to report sensitive conduct concerns.

When reporting an incident, provide as much relevant information as possible, including:

* What happened.
* When and where it happened.
* Who was involved.
* Any relevant links or other evidence.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions directly associated with Robot Simulation, including:

* GitHub issues.
* Pull requests.
* Discussions.
* Code reviews.
* Project-related communication.

It may also apply to behavior outside project spaces when that behavior directly and materially affects the safety or ability of project participants to participate in the project.
